## Decision rule (fixed before running)

An interval method is **calibrated** at a horizon and level if the nominal level lies inside the 95% confidence interval of its observed coverage. That interval uses Newey–West standard errors, because hits from overlapping multi-week forecasts are correlated. Among calibrated methods, the one with the lowest mean **interval score** (Gneiting & Raftery, 2007) is chosen. The interval score charges the width plus 2/α times any miss distance, so a method cannot win by being uselessly wide or overconfidently narrow.
