## 2. Walk-forward results

`MAE / Naive` below 1 means the model beats a random walk. The DM p-value tests whether that difference is larger than chance (Diebold–Mariano with Harvey–Leybourne–Newbold correction, absolute-error loss). Coverage is the share of outcomes inside the 95% interval; a well-calibrated interval scores close to 95%.
